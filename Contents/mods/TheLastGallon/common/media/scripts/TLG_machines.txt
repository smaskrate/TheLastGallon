    /* Machines are placeable items: process state lives in item modData
       ("tlgProc", TLG.Integration.Machines) and travels with pickup.
       The fermenter stands on the floor as the mod's own model
       (TLG_models.txt) and has its own icon (Item_TLG_Fermenter.png, drawn
       by tools/make-icons.ps1); the other models, the in-hand models and
       the other icons are verified vanilla references used as placeholders
       (docs/asset-audit.csv A03/A04/I04/I09). DisplayCategory must be a
       vanilla category: the inventory shows getText("IGUI_ItemCat_" ..
       category) (ISInventoryPane.lua:2547), and "Tools" has no text
       (runs 65-66 showed the raw key). The fermenter is carried in both
       hands, scripted as vanilla's heavy items are (LargeMeteorite, the
       anvils: Tags base:heavyitem, RequiresEquippedBothHands; BC
       InventoryItem.isForceDropHeavyItem): vanilla takes it into both
       hands (ISEquipHeavyItem) and drops it at the character's feet when
       another heavy item is taken or a weapon equipped from the menus
       (ISUnequipAction) or a vehicle entered (forceDropHeavyItems);
       Machines.install follows each. An idle character draws no carried
       item but a weapon, so its
       hand model shows only during actions (RT-SP runs 73-75). Recipes use
       item ids verified in the
       42.21 item scripts; craftRecipe grammar mirrors vanilla
       generated/entities/*/craftRecipes. Tags decide where a recipe is
       offered: the crafting window and the inventory "craft" menu list only
       InHandCraft / AnySurfaceCraft recipes (ISHandcraftWindow.lua:53; BC
       CraftRecipeManager.getUniqueRecipeItems). Without them none of these
       recipes could be crafted (found 2026-10-31). Tags mirror vanilla
       MakeOilPress (AnySurfaceCraft;Carpentry), the screwdriver assemblies in
       recipes_electrical/radio (AnySurfaceCraft) and MakeMetalSheet
       (InHandCraft;Welding); decision D-019. Pots and jars are containers,
       which a recipe keeps unless the input says mode:destroy (vanilla
       pasta/rice recipes: item 1 [Base.Pot;Base.PotForged] mode:destroy);
       without it the crafts gave the pot and jars back (RT-SP run 11).
       Crafted RT-SP 2026-10-31 (run 12) through the inventory craft menu:
       fermenter, pot still, reflux still, drying column. The sampling kit,
       filter and stabilizer are listed, not crafted in game. */
