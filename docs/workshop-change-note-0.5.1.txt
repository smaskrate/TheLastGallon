[b]Version 0.5.1[/b]

Only the documents and the version number changed: the mod plays as 0.5.0 did.

[b]Corrected[/b]
• The player guide, the support matrix and the migration notes say "gasoline", as the game does, where they said "petrol".
• A wrong fuel's wear and the wear of stale fuel multiply; the guide said one adds to the other.
• A kit sample of a vehicle's or generator's tank says what the fuel it found means for the engine; the guide said nothing reads that verdict once the tank is filled.
• Diesel vehicles and diesel pumps come only with a world started as a new game with version 0.2.0 or later.
• This page says what a save loaded without the mod loses: its machines and its fluids in containers (ethanol, diesel, biodiesel, and the water and sediment in old fuel), not the ethanol alone.

The player guide (docs/player-guide.md in the mod's folder) has the steps and the numbers. docs/CHANGELOG.md lists the changes by version.
