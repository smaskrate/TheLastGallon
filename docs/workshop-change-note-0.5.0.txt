[b]Version 0.5.0[/b]

[b]New: gas generators[/b]
• A generator converted to gas runs on propane or on wood gas. "Start: Gas generator conversion", gone from the Workshop menu since 0.4.2, is back. The work stops after its first half hour until the generator's fuel is drained into a can. A converted generator takes no liquid fuel; "Remove the conversion" makes it a petrol generator again.
• Propane: "Gas (The Last Gallon)" > "Connect a propane tank" puts a tank from your bag, or from the floor beside the generator, into the generator, which burns it as it runs. "Remove the propane tank" gives it back with the gas not yet burned.
• The Wood Gasifier, welded in hand at Welding 5 from a metal drum, two sheets of metal, three metal pipes and two metal bars. Put it down within two tiles of gas generators, load it with logs, firewood, planks, twigs or charcoal and light it: after half an hour it keeps them supplied, the nearest first. It goes out three hours after it stops: out of wood, its filter full, or no generator drawing. It burns only while a player is in the area.
• The Wood Gas Handbook, found where the Field Manual is, teaches the gasifier's recipe. With it read, the game lets you make the gasifier from Welding 4; without it, a character learns the recipe at Welding 7.
• "Gas status" says how long the gas lasts at the generator's load, in whole hours. A generator on wood gas holds only the next hour's burn, so the game's Generator Info window shows it at "Fuel: 1%".

[b]New[/b]
• "Status" of a machine on the ground opens a card that stays open: it updates every ten minutes of game time, lists the machines it takes from within two tiles, and closes when you walk away or pick the machine up.
• "Status" shows how far the stage is, about how long the batch still takes, and the machine's condition. A machine wears with every batch and a gasifier with every full filter; a worn one gives less.
• "Repair", for an idle, empty machine or a cold gasifier at 75% or less, gives back a quarter of the condition. The fermenter takes a plank and two nails, with a hammer; the other machines and the gasifier a metal pipe, with a screwdriver.
• The Field Manual has "Read: Biodiesel" and "Read: Gas generators", and each handbook opens its page. Manuals and handbooks found before this version have them too.
• A vehicle's tank and a generator's have a Fuel entry that names the engine and offers "What I know about it", "Look and smell" and "Take a sample (kit)" on the fuel in the tank.
• After the game's own Add Fuel, a line over your character says when the tank now holds the wrong fuel for its engine. The refuel itself is not stopped.
• Each of the mod's 18 items has a tooltip saying what it is for and where its command is.

[b]Changed[/b]
• What an action did is said in a line over your character, with no window to close; a one-sentence refusal too, in red. Windows stay for what you read, such as "Status", and for refusals with a list.
• A machine's menu entry carries its name, such as "Pot Still (The Last Gallon)", in place of "Machine (The Last Gallon)".
• "Pour into the station reservoir" and "Drain the generator into this can" take time, like the other pours. A pump the mod reads for the first time with more than 10,000 L counts as full and refuses a pour.
• "Feed the firebox" no longer burns planks; "Feed the firebox, planks too" does.
• Loads never take an item marked as a favourite.
• The Workshop menu shows only the entries that apply to the project, and a "Start: …" entry shows what the conversion asks for.
• A greyed entry says why when the pointer is on it. Loads and collects say what they took and gave.
• "Cancel the project" and "Remove the conversion" ask before they act.

[b]Fixed[/b]
• Closing one of the mod's windows set the game to normal speed, so a "Status" read while waiting at a faster speed slowed the wait. The speed now stays as it was.
• The station pour, the pour into a generator and the drain take the pump or generator nearest you. Before, at a pair of pumps they could take the other one.
• A pour into something full was refused with the code "destination_full"; it now says "It is full: there is no room for more fuel."
• In Italian the Wood Retort, the Wash Tank and the Drying Column give their state in the feminine ("Pronta").

[b]Existing saves[/b]
• A save made with 0.4.3 continues with this version: its fuel records and a running fermenter's batch stay as they were.
• A generator converted to gas with version 0.4.1 or earlier can now take gas, once any petrol in it is drained. Not checked in game.
• Before you remove the mod, read "Removing the mod" in the player guide: a save loaded without it loses the mod's items and fluids.

[b]Checked in game[/b]
• In single player: the conversion, the tank drained, the handbook read, the gasifier made, loaded and lit, and a generator run three hours on its gas; propane for four hours, through a save and a trip; eight hours asleep beside a wood-gas generator without a stop; one gasifier feeding two generators; the Status card and Repair; the tooltips and the manual's new pages, in English and Italian.
• On a dedicated server, on one computer: propane; the gasifier from two players, a player joining while it burns and a server restart; with one player, the Status card, the tank entries, the drain and the station pour.
• This version's package installed clean and played. With the same package: a save made with 0.4.3 continued; a save played without the mod gained it; the mod taken off a save and put back, in a save without a gasifier or a gas generator.

[b]Not checked in game[/b]
• The conversion refused without its skills or recipe; a gas generator switched on from its own menu; how long a propane tank lasts; wet wood; a filter filled by real hours of burning; the Generator Info window of a generator on gas.
• On screen: the lines over your character and what an entry shows under the pointer. Status's progress and time left beyond a fermentation just begun.
• The station pour in single player; the time and look of a drain; the wrong-fuel line after a refuel at a pump; a load with planks or a favourite carried; the questions before "Cancel the project" and "Remove the conversion".
• On a server: "Repair", the Workshop menu and the manual's new pages. A real network.

The player guide (docs/player-guide.md in the mod's folder) has the steps and the numbers. docs/CHANGELOG.md lists the changes by version.
