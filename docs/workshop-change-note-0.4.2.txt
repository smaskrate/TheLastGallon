[b]Version 0.4.2[/b]
Changes since 0.4.1, the version uploaded here before. Tested on Project Zomboid 42.22.2.

[b]Fixed[/b]
• A generator's Workshop menu offered "Start: Gas generator conversion". A generator converted to gas takes gas only, and nothing in the mod gives it gas yet, so it could not run. The entry is gone.

[b]Existing saves[/b]
• A gas conversion begun with an earlier version stays as it is. "Cancel the project" ends one in progress and returns three quarters of the parts handed over. "Remove the conversion" turns a converted generator back into a petrol generator and returns half of its parts.

[b]Not checked in game[/b]
• A server.
• "Cancel the project" and "Remove the conversion", for any conversion.

The player guide (docs/player-guide.md in the mod's folder) has the steps and the numbers. docs/CHANGELOG.md lists the changes by version.
