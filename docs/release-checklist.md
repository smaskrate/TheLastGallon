## Open implementation items

* Adapters, placeable machines and UI for biodiesel, wood gas, biogas,
  propane cylinders and electrical networks (domains merged and tested).
* Automation wiring (rules exist in the domain; no adapter yet).
* "Prepare removal" admin command (`migrations.md`). Run 54 shows what it
  must cover: without the mod, the game drops its machines (with their
  batches) and its ethanol in containers when it loads the save (its other
  fluids not tried; run 55 the same). Until then the player guide and
  `migrations.md` say what is lost and what to use up first.
* Wood-gas and electric vehicle conversions (no shipped definitions).
* Mark the making stages (`makes`) of the biodiesel, biogas, wood retort
  and ash pit recipes with their machines; until then Abort counts every
  stage of them (D-023).
* The fermentation vessel carried during an action that plays its own
  animation: the action replaces the carry pose on the arms' layer, and
  the barrel follows the action's prop bone (run 96, "Loot": tilted up
  beside the head until the action ends). Placing it plays no animation
  and keeps the pose. To do: hide the barrel or keep the pose during such
  actions. Also running, sneaking and multiplayer with the carry pose,
  and the supplied model's editable sources (a `.blend` file or the
  Tripo project; `art-src/fermenter/PROVENANCE.md`).
