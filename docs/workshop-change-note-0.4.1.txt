[b]Version 0.4.1[/b]
Changes since 0.1.1, the version uploaded here before. Tested on Project Zomboid 42.22.2.

[b]New[/b]
[list]
[*]Biodiesel. Five new machines turn wood and cooking oil into biodiesel: the Wood Retort, the Ash Barrel, the Lye Leacher, the Biodiesel Reactor and the Biodiesel Wash Tank. Each takes what it needs from the finished machine beside it, and the wash tank fills a fuel can.
[*]Building them takes skill. The recipes ask Welding 4 for the Wood Retort and the Wash Tank, Welding 5 for the Reactor, Welding 2 for the Ash Barrel, Carpentry 2 for the Lye Leacher. With the handbook read, the game lets you build each from one level below, as it does for its own recipes read from a magazine. The recipes are learned from the Biodiesel Handbook, found in gas stations, garages, farms, bookstores, tool stores and libraries, or by reaching two levels above the level a recipe asks.
[*]The Diesel Generator, found in loot beside the game's generators (the green one). It burns diesel and biodiesel in any blend.
[*]Diesel vehicles and pumps, in new worlds only: pickup vans, six-seat vans, radio vans and ambulances are diesel, and about one station pump in four gives diesel. "Read what this pump gives", in a pump's menu, says which.
[*]The wrong fuel damages an engine: diesel or biodiesel in a petrol or converted (flex) engine, petrol or ethanol in a diesel, ethanol past the limit of an engine that is not converted. The more of it is in the tank, the worse the engine starts and runs and the faster it wears. With enough of it some engines do not start, and each try costs engine condition. Diesel in a petrol car wears its exhaust too. A diesel with more than half petrol, or more than a tenth ethanol, in its tank can fail while it runs.
[*]A line over your character says when an engine starts on fuel that harms it, when a start damaged it, and when it failed. "Pour into this vehicle's tank" and "Pour into the generator" refuse a pour that would harm the engine until you confirm. The game's own Add Fuel and pumps do not check.
[*]New sandbox option "Wrong fuel damages engines", on by default. Off, such fuel only stops the engine from starting.
[/list]

[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*]The mod's right-click entries showed where nothing of theirs was clicked. The Workshop entry showed for any click while a car or a generator was near your character; it now shows when you click the car, or the generator or its tile. The Fuel and Machine entries showed for a click anywhere on the tile of a can or a machine; they now show where the game's own Grab entry for it shows, on it or close beside it.
[*]A generator whose fuel the mod had recorded was taken for a station pump, so the Fuel menu near it could offer a pump's entries. The same is fixed for a fixed container such as a barrel.
[*]A placed Diesel Generator lost its picture once its save had been loaded without the mod, and putting the mod back did not bring it back. Now the picture comes back when the game loads the generator's tile.
[/list]

[b]Existing saves[/b]
[list]
[*]A save from an earlier version continues with this one: its fuel records stay, and its vehicles and pumps stay on petrol. Diesel vehicles and pumps appear only in worlds started with 0.2.0 or later. Checked from a 0.1.2 save; 0.1.2 differs from 0.1.1 only in its version number and documents.
[*]An engine holding the wrong fuel, which earlier versions only kept from starting, now starts, or tries to, and is damaged. Siphon or drain the tank first.
[*]If you played version 0.2.0 to 0.3.2, installed from its zip: a character who could build them must now learn the recipes, from the handbook or by skill. Machines already built keep working.
[*]Before you remove the mod, read "Removing the mod" in the player guide: a save loaded without the mod loses the mod's machines and the mod's fluids, ethanol, diesel and biodiesel among them, and a placed Diesel Generator has no picture until the mod is back.
[/list]

[b]Not checked in game[/b]
[list]
[*]Driving in the cold, and diesel below -8 °C.
[*]On a server: driving on any of these fuels, the cold, a converted engine with diesel, and a repair after wrong fuel.
[*]The Lye Leacher in the crafting window on a server, and at Carpentry 1.
[*]The menu fix by a player's own click and with a controller.
[/list]
